- **Pricing / cost (topic gap — biggest win):** NONE of the ranking pages show any pricing or ranges. A short, honest "what a landscape build costs in Calgary / what drives the price" section is genuine information gain and a trust magnet.
- **Service-area depth (depth gap):** Great North lists only "Calgary." Name every community you serve (Calgary + quadrants, Airdrie, Cochrane, Chestermere, Okotoks). Tazscapes does this and it helps them.
- **Build-quality explainer (quality gap):** No competitor explains *how* they build. Your open-grade base prep + Techo-Bloc materials is a concrete, differentiating proof point — turn it into a short "how we build it to last" section.
- **Owner-run trust (quality gap):** Competitors use faceless stock copy. A named, owner-run team (Ashton, Cayden & Keagan Madden), "fully insured and guaranteed," reads as more trustworthy and is unique.
- **FAQ (topic gap vs Great North):** Great North has none. Tazscapes' FAQ is good; match and beat it with a cost question answered honestly.
- **Cross-season angle:** You're landscaping *and* snow — one contractor year-round. No pure-landscaping competitor can say this.
